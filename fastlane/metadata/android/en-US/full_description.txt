Field helps coaches, PE teachers and fitness professionals run fitness testing for their athletes, right where the testing happens. It works completely offline, so you can use it on a sports ground with no signal.

<b>Run a testing event</b>
• Pick a group and the tests you need, then record results athlete by athlete in a simple grid.
• Time runs and sprints with the built-in stopwatch, one athlete at a time or a whole heat at once, with multiple trials.
• Use the counter and keypad for reps, distances and measurements.

<b>See what each result means</b>
Every result is graded against age- and sex-specific norms drawn from published exercise-testing standards, including ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription. Results fall into three clear zones:
• Green: Superior (80th percentile and above)
• Yellow: Healthy (40th to 79th percentile)
• Red: Needs Improvement (below the 40th percentile)
Norms are bracketed for childhood, adolescence, adults and older adults.

<b>Track progress over time</b>
• Athlete profiles with history charts and a fitness radar across strength, endurance, speed, flexibility and more
• Event reports, group trends and leaderboards
• A list of athletes who need attention, so you know where to focus
• Export reports to share with athletes, parents or colleagues

<b>Built for coaches</b>
• 83 fitness tests with instructions, most with a demonstration video
• Create your own tests, with optional scoring bands
• Organise athletes into groups, classes or squads
• Starts with a small sample team so you can explore before adding your own athletes

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
• Everything is stored on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Optional backup to your own Google Drive, restorable on a new phone.

Field is free and open source (MIT licence), developed at Azim Premji University, Bengaluru, as a contribution to sports education.
