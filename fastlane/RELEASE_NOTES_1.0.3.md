## Field 1.0.3

Install: download **Field-1.0.3.apk** below and open it on your Android phone or tablet (Android 8.0 or newer). It updates an existing install and keeps all your athletes, groups and results.

### Testing
- The stopwatch keeps the athlete's time on screen after STOP.
- Rep and count tests have a number keypad, and a count of zero can be saved.
- The Beep and Yo-Yo stage picker stops at the tests' real level and shuttle limits.
- The 1 km Run accepts times over 10 minutes (up to 25 minutes).
- Group heats, Quick Test, Create Event and the group leaderboard: several fixes found in a testing-workflow review.

### Reports
- The "Healthy" line on an athlete's test history chart is drawn at the real Healthy cut-off (it was drawn at the bottom of the scale).
- The Event Report no longer hides group events after many Individual Tests.
- The radar chart updates when results change and includes custom tests.
- The Peers sheet lists each athlete once.
- Switching events in the Event Report no longer flips back to the previous one.

### Roster, Library and Settings
- Start Quick Test works when an athlete is opened from the Roster.
- Deleting an athlete says how many recorded results will be removed with them.
- Restoring a Drive backup from Settings restores the coach's name.
- The "test deleted" message in the Tests library appears at the bottom and disappears on its own.
