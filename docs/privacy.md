# Privacy Policy for Field

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Field is a free, open-source Android app for coaches and PE teachers to record fitness test
results. It is built to work offline. This policy explains what data the app handles and where
that data goes.

## The short version

- Everything you enter stays **on your device**.
- The developer runs **no servers** and **receives none of your data**.
- There are **no ads, analytics or tracking** of any kind.
- If you turn on Google Drive backup, your backup goes to **your own Google Drive** and nowhere else.

## Data stored on your device

Field stores the following in a private database on your phone or tablet. Other apps cannot read it.

- **Your coach profile:** name, username, an optional email address, and your password. The
  password is stored only as a one-way hash.
- **Athletes:** name, date of birth, sex, and optional email, medical-alert notes and general notes.
- **Groups, testing events and test results.**

Android's automatic device backup is turned off for Field, so this data is not copied anywhere
unless you use Google Drive backup yourself.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to back up, Field asks you to sign in with Google and requests two permissions:

- **Your email address**, so the app can show which Google account the backup is linked to.
- **Field's own app-data folder in your Google Drive** (`drive.appdata`). This is a hidden folder
  that only Field can read. Field cannot see any of your other Drive files.

The backup file holds the same data listed above. It is stored in your Google Drive account,
under Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer cannot access
it.

**To delete a backup:** open Google Drive on the web, go to **Settings → Manage apps → Field →
Options → Delete hidden app data**. You can also revoke Field's access at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Field's use of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Demonstration videos

Some tests include a demonstration video from YouTube. When you open a test's video or
thumbnail, your device loads it directly from YouTube, and YouTube's
[Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to that request. Nothing you have
entered in Field is sent to YouTube.

## Athletes' data and children

Field is for coaches and teachers, and is not meant to be used by children. Coaches often record
data about minors, including dates of birth and medical notes. You are responsible for getting
any consent that your school, club or local law requires before you record it.

## Deleting your data

To delete everything Field stores on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage in
Android settings. To delete a Drive backup, follow the steps above.
